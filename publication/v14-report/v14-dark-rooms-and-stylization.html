1. Executive Summary

Two completed stages:

  1. Dark Rooms Fix — boosted hallway and bathroom lighting: 19/23 → 22/23 PASS
  2. Phase F: Gemini Stylization — AI stylization of all 23 renders into Japandi photorealism: 23/23 success

2. Dark Rooms Fix

2.1 Problem

4 cameras from v13 had WARNING status:

  • hallway_01/02/03 — brightness 0.099–0.114, black% 39–41% (no windows, only LED cove + 2 downlights)
  • bathroom_01 — brightness 0.099 (camera was facing a dark wall)

2.2 Solution

Lighting (lighting_config.json updated, 39 → 45 fixtures):
