## Key Themes & Recommendations

### Top Trends
1. **AI Agent + Obsidian is the dominant 2026 narrative** — Claude Code integration is the most-discussed topic
2. **Minimalist plugin approach** — community shifting from "install everything" to curated 5-6 essential plugins
3. **Bases (core plugin)** replacing Dataview for many use cases
4. **Notebook Navigator** as a rising star plugin
5. **NotebookLM as research companion** — feeding Obsidian content into NotebookLM for audio/summaries

### Recommended Plugins (from research)
- Templater (template automation)
- Dataview / Bases (queries and databases)
- Excalidraw (visual diagrams)
- Notebook Navigator (unified workflow)
- Calendar (daily notes navigation)
- Digital Garden (free publishing alternative)

### Recommended Workflows
- **PARA structure** (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive) — still the most recommended organizational method
- **Daily notes + Templater** for capture
- **Linking Your Thinking (LYT)** framework by Nick Milo for knowledge organization
- **Claude Code agent** reading/writing vault files for task automation
- **Quartz 4** for free static site publishing (alternative to Obsidian Publish)

### Channels to Follow
- **Linking Your Thinking (Nick Milo)** — 317K subs, gold standard for Obsidian education
- **Karlos Obsidian Tutorials** — 6.8K subs, deep dives and CEO vault analysis
- **Focus Cafe** — 1.4K subs, excellent plugin reviews
- **Chase AI** — 63K subs, Obsidian + AI integration content
- **ИИшенка** — 17K subs, Russian AI + Obsidian automation
